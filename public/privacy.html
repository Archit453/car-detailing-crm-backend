Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 | Application: Creation India / Signature Detailing CRM (@creationindia_)

1. Introduction & Overview

Creation India ("we," "our," or "us"), operating the business development and customer communications system for Signature Detailing (Instagram: @creationindia_), is committed to safeguarding your privacy and personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes how our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform and messaging automation systems collect, use, process, and protect your information when you interact with us via Instagram Direct Messages (DMs), WhatsApp, or our web applications.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you explicitly choose to provide when contacting us for vehicle detailing, growth inquiries, or customer support:

  • Direct Message Content: Text messages, questions, and inquiries sent via Instagram Direct Messages or WhatsApp.
  • Public Profile Details: Your Instagram username, display name, and unique numeric Instagram-Scoped User ID (IGSID) assigned by Meta.
  • Customer Contact Details: Name, phone number, vehicle details, and selected service package provided voluntarily during quote preparation or appointment scheduling.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To respond automatically and manually to your inquiries regarding detailing packages (PPF, Ceramic Coating, Paint Correction, Detailing).
  • To provide accurate cost estimates, slot availability, and garage appointments.
  • To maintain customer relationship records and follow-up communication requested by you.
  • To improve automated response quality and customer support turnaround time.
Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We do not sell, lease, trade, or monetize your personal data with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

Your data is hosted in high-security database facilities managed by Supabase and Vercel cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard encryption protocols:

  • Encryption in Transit: All API communication with Meta Graph API and CRM endpoints is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / SSL.
  • Access Control: Administrative dashboard access is strictly restricted through authenticated session cookies and role-based access control.
  • Data Retention: Conversation logs are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill business transactions and warranty support, after which they are archived or deleted.

5. Compliance with Meta Platform Terms

Our integration strictly conforms to Meta's Developer Terms and Platform Policies:

  • We only access direct message endpoints after receiving express user interaction (inbound DM).
  • All tokens and Meta Graph API interactions are securely managed in protected environment variables and never exposed to the public browser.
  • We respect Meta's data minimization policies and only request necessary permissions (instagram_basic, instagram_manage_messages).

6. User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request the complete deletion of all personal data and message history stored by our CRM.

Option 1: Request Deletion via Email (Processed within 24 Hours)

You can revoke third-party app access directly within the Instagram app at any time:

  • Open Instagram → Go to Settings and privacy.
  • Navigate to Website permissions or Apps and websites.
  • Find the application and tap Remove / Revoke Access.
